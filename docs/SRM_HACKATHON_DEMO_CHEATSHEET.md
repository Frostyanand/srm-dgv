### 🚀 Recommended Live Demo Flow (3–5 Minutes)

1. **Student Initiation & Template Selection**:
   - Log in as **Rahul Sharma** (`rahul.ctech@srmist.edu.in`).
   - Navigate to **"Apply / Submit Doc"**.
   - Click on the **"Hackathon Organization Permission"** or **"On-Duty (OD) / Medical Leave"** template.
   - Show how the system auto-locks **Dr. S. Karthik (Faculty Advisor)** at **Stage 1 (FA Gatekeeper)**.
   - Upload any test PDF and click **Submit**.
   - Go to **"Track My Requests"** to show the live visual progress bar currently waiting at **Stage 1: Dr. S. Karthik**.

2. **Faculty Advisor Gatekeeper Review**:
   - Log out, and log in as **Dr. S. Karthik** (`fa.ctech.secA@srmist.edu.in`).
   - Navigate to **"Pending Approvals"**.
   - Review the document, enter remarks (e.g., *"Recommended for technical symposium participation"*), and click **Approve & Sign**.
   - Emphasize the **ECDSA digital signature generation** in real-time.

3. **Academic Advisor Stage**:
   - Log out, and log in as **Dr. A. Rajesh** (`aa.ctech.3rd@srmist.edu.in`).
   - Show how the document now appears in their queue ONLY after the FA passed it through the gatekeeper!
   - Approve & sign with remarks.

4. **HOD / Dean Stage & Completion**:
   - Log in as **Dr. M. Murali** (`hod.ctech@srmist.edu.in`) or **Dr. Revathi** (`dean.soc@srmist.edu.in`).
   - Sign off the final approval.
   - The workflow marks as **COMPLETED**, generating the immutable SHA-256 ledger entry.

5. **Student Verification & Download**:
   - Switch back to **Rahul** (`rahul.ctech@srmist.edu.in`).
   - Show the **Track My Requests** page: all stages turned green, view the complete chronological audit trail with cryptographic signature hashes, and click **Download**!
